# Client certificate

## Rotate certificate

Before the certificate expires, you will receive a message that the certificate is expiring soon in x days.

![Client Expiring](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-01.png)

To rotate the certificate, go to the Central View Settings under **Configuration & Activity**.
Click the **Rotate Certificate** button.

![Rotate Certificate](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-02.png)

By default the **Generate a new Certificate** is selected.
Change the validity period (default is 3 years).
Click **Next**

![Generate a new Certificate](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-03.png)

Review your new settings, click **Finish** to apply the new settings.

![Click Finish](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-04.png)

When the popup shows, you can click **Close**

![Click Close](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-05.png)

Click **Save**.

![Click Save](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-06.png)

A new window will appear to rotate the new certificate on one or more machines.
Select a specific Machine Group or **All Machine Groups**

![Select Machines](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-07.png)

Click **OK** to start the certificate rotation.

![Click OK](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-08.png)

Click **OK** to close

![Click OK](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-09.png)

To view the status, click **Show rotation status** on the **Machines & Inventory** tab.

![alt text](images/client-certificate/client-certificate-10.png)
